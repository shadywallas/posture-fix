Privacy policy

Posture & Neck Pain Exercises (Posture Fix)
Effective date: November 13, 2026

The app does not collect any data. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server, and the app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code.

What stays on your device

Your hold, rest and repetition settings, your voice choice and your session history are stored on your iPhone and, if you use one, your Apple Watch. They are not uploaded anywhere. The iPhone and the Apple Watch share them with each other directly, over the connection Apple provides between your own devices.

Apple Health

If you do a routine with an Apple Watch, the app can save it as a workout to the Health app on your own devices. The app asks for your permission before it saves workouts to Apple Health. The workout goes to your Health app only. The developer cannot see it, and the app does not send it anywhere else.

During a routine with the Apple Watch, the app reads your heart rate and active energy from Apple Health. They are kept only in the session history on your own devices and are not sent anywhere else.

Camera and other sensors

During a routine, the Apple Watch records the motion of your wrist (the angle of your arm and how steady it is) to show in your session history. This motion data is kept only on your own iPhone and Apple Watch.

The app does not use the camera. It does not use your location and does not ask for your contacts or photos.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your settings and session history stay on your devices until you delete them. Deleting the app from your iPhone and Apple Watch removes them. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay in the Health app, where you can view or delete them. You can turn off the app's access to Apple Health at any time in the Health app: tap your profile picture, then Apps under Privacy. Because the developer receives no data, there is no copy held elsewhere to delete.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at https://github.com/shadywallas/posture-fix/issues.
