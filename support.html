Posture & Neck Pain Exercises (Posture Fix)

Using the Posture Fix routine

  1. Open the app on your iPhone and tap Start on the Routine tab. You do not need an Apple Watch.
  2. Each exercise shows its instruction and gives you a moment to get into position.
  3. The app says "Hold" and "Rest" and shows a large countdown. When the last hold is done it says "Done".
  4. After each exercise the app shows which one is next and waits. Tap Start when you are ready.
  5. You can pause, resume or end at any time.

The routine has four exercises: Arms Out (T), Arms Up (Y), Elbows Back (W) and Upside-Down Y. The defaults are a 10 second hold, a 3 second rest and 10 repetitions. Change them before an exercise begins, and the app remembers them for each exercise.

Apple Watch (optional)

With an Apple Watch the app adds vibration, heart rate and calories. A routine done with the watch is saved to Apple Health as one workout. Start, pause and end work from either device.

Voice

The voice setting offers Automatic, Watch and Phone. Cues are spoken on one device at a time. If you hear nothing, check the media volume on the device that is set to speak, and that the speaker button on the run screen is not muted.

Quick timer

The Quick timer is a simple hold and rest timer on iPhone and Apple Watch, separate from the routine.

History

Completed sessions and totals are listed in the app on your iPhone.

Medical note

This app does not replace medical advice. If you have an injury or persistent pain, consult a healthcare professional.
